double_top  /U+DU+D/  -> 1 occurrence(s) (regex + numeric peak check)
    Two peaks of *similar height* separated by a trough: the up/down *shape* (U+DU+D roughly) is regular, but 'similar height' compares actual price magnitudes across candles, which needs a numeric check outside the finite automaton. Handled as: FA flags the U+DU+D shape as a *candidate*, then a small numeric post-filter checks the two peak highs are within ~1.5% of each other.
